# Blademaster links

Generated by `bun wisp combos --links --fighter Blademaster`; do not edit. Each opener hit, by region class, lands on Rifleman at the stage's centre with its weight set to 60, 75, 100, 143 (Melee units; Fox is 75), at 0–150% before the hit, under DI out, up, down, in, none. Each region is measured from two setups, the one that leaves the attacker the most frame advantage and the closest one that leaves it any, an aerial with and without drifting toward the victim, and each follow-up keeps the better class, then the fewer DIs that escape. For each follow-up the combo explorer's attacker inputs, plus a grab out of a pivot after a dash away of 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 frames and a hop out of a special on the first frame its jump cancel opens, are searched for a hit that lands at most 12 frames after the victim can act, and the 4 earliest of each input from each start frame are replayed against every victim option of the agency measure that changes where the victim ends up.

Classes, over the DIs the opener does not kill through: T true (every DI and every option is hit), DI DI-dependent (every option is hit under some DI, and another DI escapes or leaves an option that escapes), R read (it lands under some DI, but under each an option other than DI escapes), · none (no DI lets it land). Frame advantage is the victim's first actionable frame minus the attacker's, without DI; KO marks an opener that takes the stock without DI. Launch angle is the victim's launch in degrees above the horizontal away from the attacker, without DI, when the hit lifts it off the ground; KO at the ledge replays the hit with the victim 60 units inside the right ledge. On shield the victim holds a full shield: frame advantage (the victim's first shield grab minus the attacker's first actionable frame, after the last hit) and shield damage, or break. The rows in the `.jsonl` beside this page add each follow-up's input, contact frame and escapes.

No setup lands a hit with up tilt, up smash, back air, up air, side special, up special, down special, so they have no rows.
